The free stay-over welcome pack

To-go gift-bag card

For partings that feel generous.

Open the printable PDF →

A filled tote by the door sends your guest off warm. This card tells them what's in it and adds one rule: take two.

How to use it

  1. Print and fill in where the bag lives (the door hook, the hallway bench).
  2. Write a short personal note at the bottom — three sentences maximum, it lands better.
  3. Restock the tote after every visit; the host checklist has you covered.

Where to put it

Propped against the tote bag by the door, or inside it where it can't be missed.

What to fill in

Bag location and a short handwritten note. The supply list is checkbox-ready.

Free, local, and unbranded. Built with love for the art of hosting.
