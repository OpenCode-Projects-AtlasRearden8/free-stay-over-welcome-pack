Help-yourself kitchen labels

Permission, posted gently, so nobody has to ask.

Open the printable PDF →

Guests never quite believe they can have the good snacks. Eight cut-out labels say it out loud, kindly, right where these debates happen.

How to use it

  1. Print on paper or, for durability, card stock.
  2. Cut along the dashed borders — no straightedge needed, slight wonk is fine.
  3. Tape or tack them to the spots they describe: coffee shelf, snack shelf, fridge, sink.

Where to put it

Wherever each label's message applies; the kitchen likes you.

What to fill in

Only the eggs and trash-day labels have blanks. The rest are ready as-is.
