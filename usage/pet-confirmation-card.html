Pet confirmation card

For the guest whose dog is basically family.

Open the printable PDF →

Pets travel, too. This card confirms the fine print of a pet stay: potty spots, bags, furniture rules, and who to call — so tails wag and nobody tenses up.

How to use it

  1. Print and fill in before the visit, or fill it in together as a nice arrival ritual.
  2. The 'good with other animals' line matters if you have a resident pet.
  3. Leave it on the counter with the water bowls.

Where to put it

The counter or table where the leash and water bowls naturally land.

What to fill in

Pet name, potty spot, bag location, furniture rules, and a backup contact keep everyone calm.
