Wi-Fi & house rules sheet

The two questions every guest has, answered once.

Open the printable PDF →

Wi-Fi and house rules are the first conversation every guest starts. Write them down once and nobody has to interrupt you to ask about the password again.

How to use it

  1. Print one copy and write the network name and password in the big dotted boxes.
  2. Tip: test the password yourself before your guest arrives — capital letters count.
  3. Tape it to the fridge or lean it on the entry table where it can't be missed.

Where to put it

The fridge or the entry table — somewhere the guest passes a dozen times a day.

What to fill in

Fill in the blanks: quiet hours, shoes, keys, and any rule of your own for this visit.
