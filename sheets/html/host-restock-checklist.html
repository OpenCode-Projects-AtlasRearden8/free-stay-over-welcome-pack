Host restock checklist

Ten minutes before every arrival. Five minutes after every goodbye. That's the whole price of hosting well.

After every stay

  • Wash towels and bedding within a day.
  • Restock toilet paper and hand soap.
  • Wipe the counters and the bedside table, and reset the welcome card.
  • Refill coffee, tea, and sugar.
  • Empty all trash and recycling.
  • Restock the to-go bag: water, snacks, the little things.
  • Sweep the guest room for strays: chargers, socks, a forgotten book.

Before guests arrive

  • Print a fresh welcome pack (bedside card, Wi-Fi & rules, towels note).
  • Refresh the neighborhood eats shortlist.
  • Test the Wi-Fi with the printed password.
  • Set the quiet hours note where a tired traveler will read it.
  • Pet coming? Fill out the pet confirmation card.

Shopping list

When the shelf is stocked and the bed is ready, you're done. Go relax; you've already been generous.
